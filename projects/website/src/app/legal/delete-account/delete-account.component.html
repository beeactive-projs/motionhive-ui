This page explains how to delete your MotionHive account, run by HIVECRAFT S.R.L., and what happens to your information afterwards. It covers both deleting your whole account and deleting only part of your data while keeping the account. MotionHive is the app published on Google Play and the App Store, and the website at https://motionhive.fit.

1. Delete your account

Send an email to contact@motionhive.fit from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account".

Writing from your own sign-in address is how we confirm the request is really yours. If you signed up with Google or Facebook, use the email address attached to that account. If you can no longer access that mailbox, write to us anyway and we will ask you for another way to prove the account is yours.

We confirm by email when we receive your request, and again once the deletion is done. You do not need to give a reason, and we will not try to talk you out of it.

2. How long it takes

We deactivate your account within 72 hours of confirming your request, so you stop appearing to other people straight away. We then permanently erase your personal information within 30 days.

Backups are kept on a rolling schedule and are overwritten within 90 days. Until a backup rolls over, your information may still exist inside it, isolated and not used for anything.

3. What gets deleted

Everything below is permanently erased:

  • Your account: name, email address, password, phone number, city and country, profile photo and profile handle.
  • Any Google, Facebook or Apple sign-in linked to your account.
  • Your coach profile, if you have one: biography, specialisations, experience, social links and venues.
  • Your workout history: logged workouts, sets, personal records and any notes you wrote.
  • Training programs and routines you created, and programs assigned to you.
  • Your group memberships, posts and comments, and your session bookings.
  • Your messages, and your relationships with coaches or clients.
  • Your saved payment details. These are held by Stripe, not by us, and we ask Stripe to delete them too.

4. What we have to keep, and for how long

Romanian law requires us to keep some records even after you leave. We keep only these, we do not use them for anything else, and they are deleted when the period below ends.

What How long Why
Invoices and payment records, including the name and amount on them 5 years Romanian accounting and tax law
A record that your account was deleted, and when 3 years Proof that we honoured your request
Records tied to an open dispute, chargeback or legal claim Until it is resolved Defending or settling the claim

Content you posted in a group stays visible to that group, but it is anonymised: your name and photo are removed, so it is no longer linked to you. If you want a specific post or comment removed as well, say so in your email and we will delete it.

5. Delete some of your data, keep your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove information. Email contact@motionhive.fit and tell us what you want removed. The same 30-day timeline applies.

People most often ask us to delete their workout history, their posts and comments, their messages, their coach profile while keeping the account, or their profile photo. Anything else you can name, we will look at.

You can also edit or remove most of this yourself inside the app, under your profile, without writing to us at all.

6. Get a copy before you go

Deletion cannot be undone, and we cannot bring your training history back afterwards. If you want to keep it, ask us for a copy of your data in the same email and we will send it to you before anything is deleted.

7. If something goes wrong

If we have not replied within 30 days, or you are not happy with how we handled your request, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP, at dataprotection.ro.
